Common Questions

Everything You Want to Know

What age do I need to be to join Funngro?

Funngro is open to teens and young adults aged 14 to 25. If you're under 18, you'll need parental approval during registration — Funngro manages this safely and simply.

Do I need prior experience or skills?

No experience is required to start. Funngro has projects for all skill levels, plus learning resources to help you improve. Many top earners started with zero experience.

How and when do I get paid?

Payments are instant upon work approval. Withdraw directly to any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or Indian bank account at any time — there are no withdrawal delays.

How much time do I need to commit?

You decide. Work as much or as little as you want around your studies. Many teens do 2–5 hours a week and still earn a meaningful income. There are no minimum hour requirements.

Are the projects and companies real and verified?

Yes. All companies on Funngro are verified before posting projects. You'll work on real briefs with real deliverables used by actual businesses — nothing fake or simulated.

What certificates will I receive?

You receive a Funngro certificate for every completed project, plus skill-specific certifications from Funngro Academy. These carry real weight on college applications and resumes.
